About

Hi, I'm Suzelaine.

I started Fern & Fine Print because insurance paperwork makes smart people feel silly, and it shouldn't. I write plain-English guides to health coverage, medical bills, and life insurance for people who are tired of jargon and sales pressure.

Everything here is general education: the words, the documents, and the questions worth asking. Fern & Fine Print is a separate education business. It doesn't sell, quote, or recommend insurance.

Author disclosure: Suzelaine Similien is a licensed insurance agent (NPN 16685113). This is shared for transparency; Fern & Fine Print does not sell insurance.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is the mini-course really free?

Yes. You can read all of Insurance Without the Headache online at /course/ without signing up. The email sign-up is only for the printable PDF and lesson emails.

Will someone call me or try to sell me insurance?

No. Fern & Fine Print is education only. We don't sell insurance, give quotes, or pass your details to anyone who does.

Can you tell me which plan to pick?

We can't recommend a plan for your situation. The lessons teach you what to compare and what to ask. For free help choosing coverage, visit HealthCare.gov or your state's official marketplace.

Who is this for?

Everyday people who feel overwhelmed by health insurance, medical bills, or life coverage: first-time plan shoppers, people leaving a job, parents planning for a family, and anyone who wants to read their own paperwork with confidence.

When can I get the Coverage Confidence Kit?

Soon. The shop and member log-in aren't open yet, so nothing can be bought here today. Sign up for the free PDF and we'll let you know by email when the kit is ready.

Is this legal, tax, or financial advice?

No. It's general information to help you understand how insurance works. Plan rules vary by plan and state, so always check your own plan's documents.
