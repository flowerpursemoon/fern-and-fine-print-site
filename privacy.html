Fern & Fine Print: privacy notice

Insurance education, minus the headache.

Effective: October 31, 2026. This notice covers this website, the free "Insurance Without the Headache" course, and any optional paid guides from Fern & Fine Print, sold by Suzelaine Similien. Fern & Fine Print does not sell, quote, or recommend insurance and is separate from any insurance services.

What we collect

  • When you sign up for the free course PDF: your first name, your last name (optional), your email address, and your mobile phone number, along with the date and time you signed up.
  • Text message consent (optional): whether you ticked the box agreeing to receive course reminder texts, and when. That box is never pre-ticked, and agreeing is not a condition of getting the course or the PDF.
  • Campaign details in the link you used (for example "utm" tags or an ad click ID), and basic device and browser information.
  • If you buy a guide later: your order details. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

To give you the course PDF, send the course lessons and reminders you asked for, occasional emails about optional paid guides, and receipts for anything you buy. We use your phone number for course reminder texts only if you ticked the text consent box; if you didn't, we won't text you. We don't use course sign-ups to contact you about insurance quotes, and we don't add you to any insurance call or text list. Course sign-ups are kept separate from any insurance business.

Text messages

If you agree to texts, message frequency varies with the course schedule. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information. Please don't send medical details.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can reply STOP to any text. You can ask for a copy of your data or ask for it to be deleted by emailing support@fernandfineprint.com.

Service providers

We use providers to run this course, for example web hosting, an email and contact-list service that stores sign-ups and sends course emails (and texts, if you agreed to them), and Stripe for payments. They handle your information on our behalf. If advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel are turned on, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ads.

Contact

Questions about this notice or your data: support@fernandfineprint.com
